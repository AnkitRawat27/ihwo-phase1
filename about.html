About IHWO

A proposed independent initiative focused on helping victims access information, support and lawful routes towards justice.

WHO WE ARE

Built around victims, not power.

International Humanitarian World Organisation (IHWO) is a proposed independent initiative. Its intended purpose is to help genuine victims find reliable information, understand their options and connect with appropriate support providers.

IHWO is not currently presented as a registered international body, government agency, police force, court, intelligence service or United Nations entity. Any legal status, country presence or partnership will be described only after it is formally established and verified.

Principles we intend to follow

Independence

Decisions should be guided by evidence, law, victim safety and transparent governance — not pressure from powerful interests.

Fairness

People are treated with dignity. Allegations are not treated as proven facts; due process and presumption of innocence matter.

Accountability

Leadership, finances, partners and case-handling processes must be subject to oversight and complaints procedures.

What IHWO will not do

  • Act as police, prosecutor, judge or private investigator.
  • Threaten, expose, punish or confront alleged offenders.
  • Guarantee arrest, conviction, compensation or a particular legal result.
  • Sell victim information or charge victims for access to support.
  • Claim partnerships, credentials or country offices that have not been verified.
