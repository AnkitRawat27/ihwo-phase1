ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Built around victims, not power.

International Humanitarian World Organisation (IHWO) is a proposed independent initiative focused on helping genuine victims find information, understand options and connect with appropriate support.

WHO WE ARE

A purpose before an institution

IHWO is at an early development stage. It is not currently represented as a registered international organisation, government agency, police force, court, intelligence service or United Nations entity. Its legal structure, governance and country operations must be established and verified before being described as active.

Our guiding idea is simple: a person's access to information and referral support should not depend on their nationality, religion, caste, gender, wealth or social position.

Principles we intend to follow

Human dignity

Listen respectfully, avoid blame and protect people from unnecessary exposure or humiliation.

Fairness and due process

Take concerns seriously without treating allegations as proven facts or bypassing lawful authorities.

Independence

Make decisions based on safety, evidence, law and transparent governance—not pressure or personal interests.

Accountability

Build independent oversight, complaints processes, conflict controls and honest public reporting.

Privacy by design

Collect as little personal information as possible and never publish a person's story without informed consent.

Zero-fee victim support

Never charge victims for IHWO information or referral assistance. Membership, if introduced, will never be a condition of help.

What IHWO will not do

  • Act as police, prosecutor, judge, intelligence agency or private enforcement group.
  • Threaten, expose, punish, stalk or confront alleged offenders.
  • Guarantee arrest, conviction, compensation or any legal outcome.
  • Sell victim information or charge victims for access to IHWO support.
  • Claim registration, official status, partners, offices or results that have not been verified.
  • Accept violent groups or criminal networks as partners or give them access to victims.
