TRANSPARENCY

Trust is earned through accountability.

IHWO will report honestly about what exists, what is still being built and how resources are used.

Development-stage disclosure: IHWO does not currently claim registered charity status, audited programme results, a funded assistance programme or an established international partner network.

Commitments before operations

Victim-first funding

IHWO intends never to charge victims for its own information and referral support.

Shared governance

The proposed model includes a founder and an elected governing board, with checks, documented responsibilities and independent oversight.

Honest reporting

Only verified activities, income, expenditure and outcomes will be reported. No inflated impact claims.

Before accepting donations

IHWO must first establish an appropriate legal structure, organisational bank and accounting controls, financial oversight and applicable fundraising permissions. This website does not accept donations.

Cross-border donations must comply with the laws of the receiving and sending countries. No personal bank account will be used as a substitute for organisational controls.

Complaints and concerns

A formal independent complaints channel, safeguarding process and whistleblower protections must be established before case-handling operations begin. Until then, do not send sensitive allegations, identity documents or evidence through this website.
