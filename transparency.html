IHWO · TRANSPARENCY

Transparency

IHWO's credibility must come from honest reporting, independent oversight and responsible use of resources.

ACCOUNTABILITY

Trust is earned through transparency.

IHWO is in its initial development phase. It does not yet claim audited programme results, a registered charity status, a funded assistance programme or an established international partner network.

Victim-first funding

IHWO intends never to charge victims for its own information and referral services.

Independent oversight

Governance, complaints, conflicts of interest and financial controls must be documented before funded operations.

Honest reporting

Only verified activities, income, expenditure and outcomes will be reported. No inflated impact claims.

Before accepting donations

IHWO will first establish its legal structure, bank/accounting controls and applicable fundraising permissions. Donations will not be solicited or accepted through this website until the organisation is legally authorised and the payment process is independently reviewed.

In particular, cross-border donations must comply with the laws of the receiving and sending countries. No personal bank account will be used as a substitute for organisational controls.

Report a concern

When a formal complaints channel is established, it will be published here with clear confidentiality and response procedures. Do not submit sensitive allegations or evidence through an unsecured general contact form.
