Find the right help, where you are.

Emergency numbers, legal-aid services and victim-support organisations vary by country. Verify contact details on official sources before sharing personal information.

In immediate danger? Do not wait for an IHWO response. Contact your local emergency service now or go to a safe public place and seek help from a trusted person.
