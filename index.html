INTERNATIONAL HUMANITARIAN WORLD ORGANISATION

Justice begins when
every victim is heard.

A proposed independent initiative to help victims understand their options, find reliable support resources and navigate pathways towards justice — wherever they live.

Find support resourcesOur mission

Victims pay nothing. IHWO does not charge victims for information or referrals. We are an initiative in development, not a police, court or emergency service.

WHAT WE AIM TO DO

Support that puts victims first

We aim to connect people with lawful, appropriate and independently verified support.

◎

Be heard

Offer clear information about possible next steps and how to approach appropriate services.

⌕

Find support

Build a directory of legal-aid, victim-support and specialist organisations, with verification status clearly shown.

⚖

Pursue justice

Help people understand lawful reporting, documentation and referral pathways without promising an outcome.

OUR COMMITMENT

Dignity. Safety. Accountability.

No victim should have to pay IHWO to access its information or referral service. Assistance will be limited to services we can safely and lawfully provide.

IMPORTANT

Real help needs trusted people.

AI may help organise information and translate content, but it cannot determine guilt, replace a lawyer, investigate crimes or make decisions about a person's safety. Serious cases require qualified human review and appropriate local services.
