A proposed independent initiative to help people affected by crime, abuse, exploitation and injustice understand their options and find appropriate, lawful support.

Explore help resourcesDiscover our mission
Victims pay nothing to IHWO. Our intended information and referral support will always be free to victims. IHWO is currently a development-stage initiative, not an emergency service or law-enforcement body.
OUR PURPOSE

Support that puts people first

We aim to make reliable information easier to understand and appropriate support easier to find—without promising outcomes we cannot control.

◎

Be heard with dignity

Help people organise their questions, understand general options and prepare to speak with qualified local services.

⌕

Find appropriate support

Develop a carefully checked directory of legal-aid, victim-support and specialist organisations. Verification status will be shown honestly.

⚖

Understand lawful pathways

Share general information about documentation, reporting and referrals while respecting due process and local law.

OUR NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLE

No fee. No discrimination. No false promises.

Victims will never be charged by IHWO for access to its information or referral support. Assistance will depend on safety, local law, verified resources and available human capacity.

RESPONSIBLE SUPPORT

Real help needs trusted people.

Technology may help with translation and organising information, but it cannot determine guilt, replace a lawyer, investigate crimes or make decisions about someone's safety. Serious situations need qualified human review and appropriate local services.
