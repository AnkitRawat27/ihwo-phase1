IHWO · OUR MISSION

Our mission

To help every genuine victim find safe, lawful and understandable pathways to support and justice.

MISSION

Justice for every victim.

IHWO's proposed mission is to reduce the barriers that prevent victims from being heard and accessing appropriate support — including lack of money, information, language access or awareness of available services.

How we aim to work

01

Listen and inform

Provide accessible information and explain possible support pathways.

02

Connect responsibly

Refer people to suitable, verified local organisations and qualified professionals where available.

03

Support follow-through

Where capacity permits, help people understand referral steps and keep track of their own next actions.

04

Learn and improve

Publish honest, privacy-protective reports about work actually completed.

Who we intend to serve

Victims of crime, fraud, cybercrime, violence, exploitation, abuse and human-rights violations. Services will be phased by country and case type, according to verified resources, safety and available human capacity.

Priority: immediate danger must be directed to local emergency services. IHWO's website is not monitored as an emergency channel.
