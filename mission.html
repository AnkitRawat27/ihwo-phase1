OUR MISSION

Justice for every victim.

To reduce the barriers that prevent victims from being heard and accessing appropriate support—such as cost, lack of information, language barriers and uncertainty about where to turn.

How we aim to work

01

Listen and inform

Offer accessible general information and help people identify possible next steps.

02

Connect responsibly

Refer people to suitable local organisations and qualified professionals only when their identity, scope and safeguards have been checked.

03

Support follow-through

Where capacity permits, help people understand referral steps and keep track of their own next actions.

04

Learn and improve

Report honestly on activities actually completed, protect confidentiality and correct mistakes.

Who we intend to serve

People affected by crime, fraud, cybercrime, violence, abuse, exploitation, trafficking and human-rights violations. The intended scope is broad, with serious and urgent safety risks prioritised when services become operational.

Support will be phased by country and case type according to local law, verified resources, safeguarding and available trained human capacity.

Immediate danger: Contact local emergency services now. This website is not monitored as an emergency channel.

How we define success

Not by publicity or the number of cases claimed. Success means a person receives accurate information, reaches an appropriate service, understands the next step and is treated with dignity—while their privacy and choices are respected.
