PARTNER NETWORK

Trust must be verified.

IHWO aims to build a responsible network of victim-support organisations and qualified professionals. Applications are not yet open.

Current status

No active partner network is being claimed. Partner recruitment and verification processes are still being designed. No organisation should be treated as an IHWO partner without due diligence, a formal agreement and public confirmation.

Potential partners may include registered NGOs, legal-aid organisations, licensed lawyers, medical and mental-health providers, social workers, universities and specialist digital-safety organisations, subject to local law and review.

Proposed verification checks

  • Legal registration or professional licence, where applicable.
  • Identity and authority of the representative.
  • Relevant experience, references and clearly defined service scope.
  • Safeguarding, confidentiality and data-protection practices.
  • Complaints procedures and conflict-of-interest controls.
  • Transparent fees, with no fee charged by IHWO to victims for IHWO support.
  • Checks to prevent exploitation, recruitment, retaliation or unauthorised access to victims.

Who we will not partner with

IHWO will not accept active criminal groups, violent networks, vigilante groups or individuals seeking access to victims for exploitation, intimidation, recruitment, retaliation or publicity. Former offenders, if ever considered for a limited role, would require lawful, role-specific risk assessment and safeguards.
