IHWO · PARTNER NETWORK

Partner network

IHWO aims to build a network of accountable organisations and qualified professionals. Applications are not open until a verification process is established.

WORK WITH US

Trust must be verified.

IHWO intends to work with legitimate victim-support organisations, legal-aid providers, universities, hospitals and qualified professionals. A name or logo on a website is not proof of a partnership.

Current status: Partner recruitment and verification are being designed. No organisation should be considered an IHWO partner unless a formal agreement and public confirmation are issued.

Proposed verification checks

  • Legal registration or professional licence, where applicable.
  • Identity and authority of the representative.
  • Relevant experience, references and service scope.
  • Safeguarding, confidentiality and data-protection practices.
  • Conflict-of-interest and complaints procedures.
  • Transparent fees and confirmation that victims will not be charged by IHWO.

Who may be eligible?

Registered NGOs, legal-aid organisations, licensed lawyers, medical and mental-health providers, social workers, universities and specialist digital-safety organisations. Eligibility will depend on local law and due diligence.

IHWO will not accept active criminal groups, violent networks, vigilante groups or anyone seeking access to victims for exploitation, recruitment or publicity.
